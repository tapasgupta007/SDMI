# SDMI
Stock Market Forecasting Model
# Problem Description
Accurate forecasting of stock market dynamics has been a persistent challenge due to the complex interplay of investor sentiment, firm-level fundamentals, and macroeconomic dynamics. Conventional sentiment proxies, such as social media and online news, often suffer from noise, manipulation, and limited firm-specific granularity. 
# Proposed Study
This study introduces the SentiEcon Dynamic Market Index (SDMI), a novel multidimensional framework that integrates authentic employee sentiment data from Glassdoor reviews with key macroeconomic indicators. Leveraging recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs), sentiment signals are extracted using a lightweight DistilBERT model and modelled through a bidirectional Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) architecture, forming the proposed LDistilBERT-BiGRU model.
# Conclusion
This research expands the scope of sentiment-based financial forecasting and underscores the value of firm-specific, internally derived sentiment indicators for investors and policymakers.
